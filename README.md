# Byro (byro.ee)

**Byro is a reputation workspace for expert-led B2B teams.** It turns selected company knowledge into source-backed LinkedIn posts, with the named author making the final call.

- Website: [byro.ee](https://byro.ee)
- Company: Codes Devs OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia
- Founders: Fathin Dosunmu and Rico Soots
- Stage: invitation-only pilot, starting with LinkedIn ([join the waitlist](https://byro.ee))
- Contact: info@byro.ee

> Not to be confused with **byro (byro.cloud)**, the open-source membership administration software at [github.com/byro/byro](https://github.com/byro/byro). This repository is about Byro the B2B software company at byro.ee. The two are unrelated.

## What Byro does

Byro starts with what a company wants to be known for. From evidence the team has approved, such as customer conversations, product decisions and shipped work, it recommends the one post or response that supports that goal and shows the source behind every line.

- **Evidence before output.** Every factual sentence traces back to a source the team confirmed.
- **One goal before another draft.** Byro recommends one post that supports the company's reputation goal, rather than a stream of content.
- **The named author makes the final call.** Teammates can contribute and review. The named author edits, approves and posts from their own LinkedIn account.

Byro does not publish automatically.

## Who it is for

Expert-led B2B teams (founders, consultancies, engineering and deep tech companies) that already use LinkedIn and want what the company knows to show up there as credible, regular work.

## Research

Byro publishes its research methods and results openly.

- [Baltic LinkedIn Substance Index 2026](https://byro.ee/research/baltic-linkedin-index-2026): 2,638 LinkedIn company posts from 111 Baltic B2B companies, scored for specificity and expertise. Finding: likes and substance are unrelated (r = 0.02).
- [How we built the Baltic LinkedIn Substance Index](https://byro.ee/research/how-we-built-the-baltic-index): company list, post collection, five-model panel and checks.
- [What AI assistants believe about Byro](https://byro.ee/research/what-ai-assistants-believe-about-byro): 650 graded answers from seven AI models about our own company, with and without web search.
